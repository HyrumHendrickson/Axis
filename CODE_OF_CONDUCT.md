# Code of conduct

Axis is built by people who want useful, private, local software. This document sets out how we work together on it.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and professional, in issues, pull requests, and discussions alike.
- Argue about ideas, not people. Disagreement is welcome when it is reasoned and specific.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding that someone meant harm.
- Give and accept criticism of code and documentation graciously. The aim is better software.
- Respect the maintainers' time and their final decisions on what the project includes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or repeatedly pushing a point after a decision has been made.
- Spam, advertising, or off-topic political or religious argument.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests, and discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break this code. As a rule, a first problem leads to a private warning, and continued or serious problems lead to removal from the project.
